# MARS NEWS — Editorial Standards, Fact Verification & Attribution Charter

## 1. Editorial Integrity Charter
MARS NEWS operates under rigorous journalistic ethics to ensure that automated news processing remains factual, responsible, and compliant with intellectual property rights.

### Core Tenets:
1. **Never Invent Facts**: The platform never generates speculative quotes, unconfirmed casualty figures, fictitious dates, or unverified announcements.
2. **Attribution Transparency**: Every published report prominently displays all source publications whose reporting informed the story, linking to their original canonical URLs.
3. **Claim Evidence Trail**: Every article contains a verified claims panel with direct source excerpts.
4. **Mandatory Hold on Sensitive Subjects**: Sensationalized, volatile, or legally delicate subjects (e.g., communal tension, crime allegations, unverified breaking deaths, political bribery allegations) are never published automatically.

## 2. Risk Classification Matrix
- **LOW RISK**: Routine scientific announcements (ISRO launches), economic data (RBI policy rate announcements), sports match scores, cultural events. Eligible for auto-publishing.
- **MEDIUM RISK**: Complex corporate mergers, disputed economic statistics, local governance policy shifts. Requires claim confidence > 85%.
- **HIGH RISK**: Political corruption allegations, election controversies, criminal accusations, unverified breaking incidents. Automatic Editorial Hold.
- **CRITICAL RISK**: Communal violence, national security incidents, unverified death reports. Automatic Editorial Hold with immediate editor notification.

## 3. Corrections Policy
When a source publication updates a report or our editorial engine needs rectification, an official correction notice is published with timestamp and editor attribution. Revisions are logged in `article_revisions`.
